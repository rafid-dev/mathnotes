\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

These notes were compiled during my studies of analysis, topology and manifolds. Initially intended as a personal resource to solidify my understanding, they have since evolved into a more comprehensive document that I am sharing publicly to benefit others pursuing similar topics.

Please note that these notes are an ongoing project and will be regularly updated and refined as my studies progress. My aim is to ensure accuracy, clarity, and depth in the presentation of the material. However, as these notes remain a work in progress, they may contain gaps or areas for improvement.

I welcome suggestions, corrections, or any feedback that could improve the quality of this document. Please feel free to reach out to me via Discord at \texttt{@j.en} or by email at \texttt{vorahsan@gmail.com}. Your input is highly appreciated.

For the latest version of these notes, please visit the GitHub repository:
\begin{center}
\texttt{https://github.com/rafid-dev/mathnotes}
\end{center}
